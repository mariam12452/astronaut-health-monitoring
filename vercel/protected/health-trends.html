DATA INTERPRETATION

Understanding Your Health Trends

Daily averages may combine measurements taken during different activity states. These charts provide an overview of physiological changes over time. Deviation alerts, however, rely on comparable measurements and verified personal baseline statistics.

Health trends: Show changes in daily measurements over time.

Personal baseline: Represents an astronaut's established physiological reference measurements.

Deviation alerts: Identify sustained deviations from the astronaut's personal baseline.
